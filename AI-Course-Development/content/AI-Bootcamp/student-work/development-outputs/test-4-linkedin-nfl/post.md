The NFL has always been a league of inches — now it's becoming a league of algorithms.

AI-powered performance analytics are reshaping how teams scout, prevent injuries, and game-plan. Player tracking systems feed models millions of data points — speed, route angles, separation — surfacing insights film study alone can't reach. The biggest early win: models flagging fatigue patterns tied to soft-tissue injuries before they happen, letting trainers intervene with load management.

Draft evaluation is shifting too. Models can now project how a college player's skill set maps onto specific NFL schemes, surfacing scheme-fit value in later rounds.

The human element stays — coaches coach, players execute. But the edge goes to whoever pairs the best judgment with the best models.
